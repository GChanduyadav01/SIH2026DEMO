The Challenge

Cognitive Healthcare Barriers in Remote Communities

Elderly dementia patients in the North Eastern Region of India face unique geographic, linguistic, and infrastructural hurdles.

🧠

Memory Decline

Gradual difficulty remembering familiar family members, routines, daily medications, and local events.

🏔️

Limited Healthcare Access

Remote hill terrains and rural communities often have scarce access to specialized geriatric neurology clinics.

👨‍👩‍👧

Caregiver Challenges

Family caregivers need intuitive tools to track cognitive trends, missed medicine reminders, and daily emotional state.

❤️

Social Isolation

Elderly individuals in nuclear setups experience reduced emotional engagement and cultural memory detachment.

📍 North Eastern Regional Focus

Designed for Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh & Sikkim.

How It Works

The SmritiAI Continuous Care Cycle

A closed-loop system connecting elderly engagement, AI analysis, and caregiver peace of mind.

1
👵📱

Patient Interaction

Patient plays culturally familiar cognitive games, tracks hydration, and responds to reminders.

2
⏱️📈

Telemetry Recording

The system logs response times, accuracy percentages, memory match trials, and mood check-ins.

3
🤖⚙️

Algorithms analyze cognitive trends and dynamically adjust game difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard).

4
🩺👨‍👩‍👧

Caregiver & Triage

Caregivers and healthcare workers receive early alerts and monitor multi-patient wellbeing trends.
